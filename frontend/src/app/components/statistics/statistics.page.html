Understanding Your Metrics

Fitness (CTL)

Chronic Training Load - Your long-term fitness built up over ~6 weeks. Higher values mean you're more trained and can handle bigger workloads.

Goal: Gradually increase over time

Fatigue (ATL)

Acute Training Load - Your short-term fatigue from the last ~1 week. High values mean you've been training hard recently.

Watch: Very high ATL may indicate overtraining

Freshness (TSB)

Training Stress Balance - The difference between your fitness and fatigue (CTL - ATL).

Positive: Well-rested, ready to race

Negative: Fatigued, building fitness

Near zero: Maintaining fitness

Consistency

Measures how regularly you train. Consistent training is key to building fitness while reducing injury risk.

Goal: Aim for 70%+ consistency
