2. Data We Collect

2.1 Personal Information You Provide

When creating an account or using the App, you may provide:

  • Name
  • Date of Birth
  • Weight
  • Height
  • Running or experience level

These data help us personalize your training features and provide better performance insights.

2.2 Data Imported From Your Strava or Garmin Account

You may choose to import running-related data from your Strava or Garmin account. This may include:

  • GPS/Location data (route, distance, elevation)
  • Run duration and timestamps
  • Speed and pace
  • Heart rate and workout intensity metrics

These data are used to generate performance statistics and personalized insights within the App.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data for the following purposes and under the legal bases permitted by the GDPR:

3.1 To provide and improve the App (Article 6(1)(b) – Contractual necessity)

  • Create and manage your account
  • Generate training insights and performance analytics
  • Enable syncing and display of smartwatch data

3.2 To ensure technical functionality and security (Article 6(1)(f) – Legitimate interest)

  • Maintain and secure our systems
  • Prevent misuse and ensure service stability

3.3 To process special categories of personal data (health data)

Some of the data you upload (e.g., heart rate, fitness data) may be considered special category data under Article 9 GDPR.
We process these data only with your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)) when you register your account.
You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us or deleting your account.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Your personal and health data are stored securely in our database.
We do not share your data with any third parties, unless required by law.

All collected data will be permanently deleted no later than 1 March 2026.
You may also request deletion at any time (see Section 8).

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission
  • Secure storage practices
  • Access restrictions and authentication controls

However, no system can be guaranteed 100% secure. You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential.

6. Sharing of Data

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties.
We may only disclose data if required to comply with legal obligations or court orders.

7. International Data Transfers

Your data are stored and processed within the European Union.
We do not transfer your data outside the EU/EEA.

8. Your GDPR Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access – Obtain a copy of your personal data
  • Right to rectification – Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure (“Right to be forgotten”) – Request deletion of your data
  • Right to restrict processing – Limit how we use your data
  • Right to data portability – Request transfer of your data to another service
  • Right to object – Object to specific types of processing
  • Right to withdraw consent – At any time
  • Right to lodge a complaint – With your local data protection authority
